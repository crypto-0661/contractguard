Frequently Asked Questions

Is ContractGuard a replacement for a real lawyer?

No. ContractGuard is designed to help you identify potential risks and understand contract language before you consult with an attorney. For high-stakes or complex legal matters, we always recommend consulting a qualified lawyer. Think of us as your first line of defense — we help you know what to ask your lawyer about.

What types of contracts can ContractGuard review?

We support virtually all contract types including vendor agreements, service contracts, NDAs, employment agreements, lease agreements, partnership agreements, freelance contracts, SaaS terms of service, and more. Our AI is trained on millions of contracts across dozens of industries.

How secure is my contract data?

Security is our top priority. All documents are encrypted at rest using AES-256 and in transit using TLS 1.3. We use Supabase for secure database storage. We never share your contract data with third parties, and we do not use your contracts to train our AI models. You can delete your data permanently at any time.

How long does a review take?

Most contracts are reviewed in under 5 minutes. Complex or very long contracts (100+ pages) may take 10-15 minutes. You'll receive an email notification when your review is complete, or you can watch the progress in real-time on your dashboard.

Can I cancel my subscription anytime?

Absolutely. There are no long-term contracts or cancellation fees. You can cancel your subscription at any time from your account settings, and you'll continue to have access until the end of your billing period. We also offer a 7-day free trial on all plans so you can try ContractGuard risk-free.

Does ContractGuard work for contracts in languages other than English?

Currently, ContractGuard is optimized for contracts written in English. We're actively working on support for Spanish, French, German, and Mandarin Chinese. If you need multi-language support, please contact our team — we'd love to understand your needs better.
