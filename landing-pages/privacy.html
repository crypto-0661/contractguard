Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 31, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly: name, email address, payment information (processed by Creem, our payment processor), and contract documents you upload for review.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to provide the AI contract review service, process payments, send account-related communications, and improve our service. We do not sell your personal information.

3. Contract Document Handling

Documents you upload are processed via AI (OpenAI/Anthropic APIs) for analysis only. Documents are encrypted in transit and at rest. We do not share your documents with third parties. You may delete your documents at any time.

4. Data Storage & Security

We use Supabase for data storage with encryption at rest. All data transmission uses TLS encryption. Payment data is handled by Creem and is never stored on our servers.

5. Cookies

We use essential cookies for authentication and session management. We use Plausible Analytics for privacy-friendly analytics that do not track individual users.

6. Third-Party Services

We use Creem for payment processing, Supabase for data storage, and OpenAI/Anthropic for AI processing. Each service has its own privacy policy.

7. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time. Contact us at privacy@clausescan.co.
